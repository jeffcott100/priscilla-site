Bio

About Priscilla A. Grijalva

Priscilla A. Grijalva is a high school counselor, educational leader, author, and advocate with more than 20 years of experience supporting students at the middle and high school levels. Her work is grounded in a deep commitment to equity, access, belonging, and expanding postsecondary opportunities for each and every student. She believes that when young people are seen, supported, and provided with clear pathways to success, their lives and the communities around them can be transformed.

Throughout her career, Priscilla has designed and led comprehensive, data-driven school counseling programs that elevate student voice, strengthen school communities, and create lasting systemic change. She has guided two schools to earn their first-ever Recognized ASCA Model Program (RAMP) designation, including one that became the first school in its district to receive the recognition. She has also helped lead districtwide initiatives focused on college and career access, equity, civic engagement, student leadership, and inclusive school culture.

Priscilla’s leadership extends beyond her school community. She has served on the American School Counselor Association Board of Directors and as a university adjunct instructor, University of California application reader, TN Achieves mentor, high school basketball coach, and host of the educational podcast Reach Higher Riverside. She has also contributed to equity-focused leadership teams and initiatives designed to strengthen belonging and educational opportunity for students.

A sought-after speaker and presenter, Priscilla has shared her work at state and national conferences, including as a main-stage speaker at the National Association for College Admission Counseling (NACAC) Conference. She has also delivered the keynote address at the University of Redlands Students of Color Commencement Ceremony. Her student-centered leadership and advocacy have been recognized at the state, national, and international levels.

Above all, Priscilla is passionate about helping each and every student recognize their potential, embrace their identities, and pursue futures they may not have imagined for themselves. Through counseling, leadership, advocacy, and education, she continues to champion systems that open doors, expand opportunity, inspire possibility, and empower young people to shape their own futures.
