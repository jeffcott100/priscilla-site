Signature Keynotes

LEVEL UP: The Courage to Lead, the Mindset to Win, and the Purpose to Make a Difference

What separates those who simply show up from those who create lasting impact? Drawing on lessons from competitive athletics, education, and advocacy, Priscilla challenges audiences to redefine success, embrace challenges, and turn passion into intentional action. Participants will leave inspired to raise the bar, lead with purpose, and elevate their impact. Are you ready to level up?

Be the Difference Maker: Never Underestimate Your Influence

Never underestimate the power of one person to change the trajectory of another person’s life. Through compelling stories and meaningful reflection, Priscilla challenges professionals to recognize their influence, reconnect with their purpose, and move beyond good intentions. Audiences will leave energized to make a lasting difference in their organizations, professions, and communities.

Opening Doors That Others Don’t Know Exist: The Power of Access, Advocacy, and Opportunity

Opportunity is not always limited by potential; sometimes it is limited by awareness, access, and systemic barriers. Drawing on her work in education and her book, Priscilla challenges audiences to examine assumptions, recognize unseen obstacles, and create pathways for others. Participants will be empowered not only to open doors but also to transform the systems that keep them closed.

Featured Presentations & Workshops

Practical strategies for educators, school counselors, leaders, and organizations committed to meaningful outcomes.

10 Lessons for Postsecondary Access

Based on her book, Priscilla shares practical systems, strategies, and lessons to expand college and career opportunities. Participants explore ways to identify access gaps, engage educational partners, and help students navigate their postsecondary journeys with confidence.

From Data to Difference

Move beyond collecting data to using it as a tool for meaningful change. Participants explore how to identify needs, establish measurable goals, evaluate outcomes, and communicate the impact of their programs to educational partners.

Advocacy in Action: From Voice to Impact

Discover how to leverage your professional voice to influence decisions, address barriers, and advocate for those you serve. Participants explore practical approaches to building relationships, communicating needs, and turning advocacy into action.

Building a College and Career-Going Culture

Explore intentional, schoolwide strategies that make postsecondary planning part of the everyday student experience. Participants identify ways to engage families, educators, and community partners while creating pathways that honor students’ individual aspirations.

The Power of Strategic Partnerships

Meaningful collaboration can expand opportunities beyond what any one professional or organization can achieve alone. Participants explore how to identify potential partners, develop shared goals, and build sustainable relationships that connect people with resources and opportunities.
